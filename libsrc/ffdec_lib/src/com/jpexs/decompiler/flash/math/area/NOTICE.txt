This package contains the OpenJDK 8 two-dimensional area implementation,
from Azul Zulu 8u512-b09 (source revision de13ce86a602). The original
copyright notices and GPL version 2 with Classpath exception apply.
The Classpath exception is extended to this modified version.

FFDec relocates the classes, renames Area to BezierArea, uses strict
floating-point arithmetic and the local OpenJDK quadratic solver, and
returns an empty area for a degenerate single-edge input. The port also adds
collection type parameters. The sweep emits finished contours as it goes and
coalesces adjoining slices on open chains instead of prematurely closing them.
